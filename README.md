# Sales Dashboard - Global Superstore 📈🌍

An advanced, interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze global business performance, sales growth, profitability, and product category contributions.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive overview of the Global Superstore's financial and operational performance[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). It helps stakeholders evaluate company health through key performance indicators (KPIs) and visual trends across multiple regions and years[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

## 🎛️ Interactive Slicers & Filters
The dashboard includes dynamic filters allowing users to slice and analyze data by:
* **Year:** 2012, 2013, 2014, and 2015[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)
* **Market:** Africa, Asia Pacific, Europe, LATAM, and USCA[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span)
* **Category:** Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)

## 💡 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Revenue:** 12.6M[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)
* **Total Profit:** 1.5M[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span)
* **No. of Orders:** 51.3K[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)
* **Overall Discount:** 14%[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span)

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations & Insights
* **Year-on-Year Sales & Profit:** Tracks business growth trends over time (2012–2015)[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
* **Category-wise Sales:** Compares total sales volume across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).
* **Category Contribution in Profit:** Breakdown of profit percentages contributed by each product category (e.g., Technology at 45.23%, Office Supplies at 39.34%, Furniture at 15.43%)[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
* **Sub-category wise Profit:** Evaluates granular profitability metrics across various product sub-categories (Storage, Paper, Labels, Copiers, Bookcases, etc.)[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).

* **Data Analysis:** Business Intelligence reporting, trend identification, and financial data structuring.
