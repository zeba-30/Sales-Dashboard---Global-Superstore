# Sales Dashboard - Global Superstore 📈🌍

An advanced, interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze global business performance, sales growth, profitability, and product category contributions.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive overview of the Global Superstore's financial and operational performance. It helps stakeholders evaluate company health through key performance indicators (KPIs) and visual trends across multiple regions and years.

## 🎛️ Interactive Slicers & Filters
The dashboard includes dynamic filters allowing users to slice and analyze data by:
* **Year:** 2012, 2013, 2014, and 2015.
* **Market:** Africa, Asia Pacific, Europe, LATAM, and USCA
* **Category:** Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.

## 💡 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Revenue:** 12.6M
* **Total Profit:** 1.5M
* **No. of Orders:** 51.3K
* **Overall Discount:** 14%

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations & Insights
* **Year-on-Year Sales & Profit:** Tracks business growth trends over time (2012–2015).
* **Category-wise Sales:** Compares total sales volume across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology
* **Category Contribution in Profit:** Breakdown of profit percentages contributed by each product category (e.g., Technology at 45.23%, Office Supplies at 39.34%, Furniture at 15.43%)
* **Sub-category wise Profit:** Evaluates granular profitability metrics across various product sub-categories (Storage, Paper, Labels, Copiers, Bookcases, etc.)

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Pivot Tables, Advanced Charts, KPI Cards, Slicers, and Professional Dashboard Styling
* **Data Analysis:** Business Intelligence reporting, trend identification, and financial data structuring.

## 📂 Project Structure
* `Global_Superstore.xlsx`: Contains the underlying data source, processing tabs (`Orders`, `Trends and Patterns`, `KPI Cards`), and the final interactive `Dashboard`
